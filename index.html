THE PERSON BEHIND ROSY

Prashant / Arbin

A Grade 9 student building a student-first space where the school routine becomes something useful, interactive and worth opening every day.

Web DevelopmentCybersecurityStudent BuilderRosy Creator
01

Why I built it

I didn't want another website that says “here is the routine” and disappears. Rosy is designed around the moments students actually need: what is next, what should I study, can I test myself, can I focus for 25 minutes, and can I see my progress?

02

What I care about

Clean interfaces, useful automation, web development and cybersecurity. The goal is to keep learning practical while making school tools feel modern instead of boring.

03

Built for students

Classes, quizzes, streaks, XP, exam alerts, teacher search, study books and focus sessions all live in one place. Your progress is saved locally on your device in this no-backend edition.
